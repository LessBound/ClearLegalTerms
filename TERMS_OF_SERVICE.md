## 3. The Service — what Clear does

Clear lets you:

- Photograph the ingredient label on a food product;
- Send the image to our backend, which uses Google's Vertex AI (Gemini) to analyse the ingredients;
- Receive a verdict — **CLEAR**, **AVOID**, or **NO_LABEL_DETECTED** — together with a list of any flagged ingredients, the detected label language, and a short summary, all evaluated against the dietary rules you have selected.

The supported dietary rules at the date of these Terms are: **Halal, Vegetarian, Vegan, Pescatarian, Beef-Free**. We may add or remove categories from time to time and will reflect any change in the in-app onboarding and Settings screens.

Clear is provided on an "as-is, as-available" basis. We do not guarantee that the Service will be uninterrupted, error-free, or that scan results will always be accurate.

## 4. AI-generated results — limitations

Scan results are generated by an AI model. By design, AI models can:

- Mis-identify or omit ingredients on a label, especially under poor lighting, in low-resolution photos, where text is partially obscured, or in languages or scripts the model handles less reliably.
- Make incorrect inferences about whether an ingredient violates your selected restrictions — for example, classifying an ambiguous emulsifier (such as "lecithin", "mono-/diglycerides", or "natural flavors") as CLEAR when in fact its source is animal-derived or non-halal.
- Return outdated or partially-correct summaries.

You agree that:

- A "CLEAR" verdict is **not a guarantee** that the product complies with your dietary requirements.
- An "AVOID" verdict is **not a guarantee** that the product is genuinely incompatible with your dietary requirements — it may be a false positive on an ingredient with multiple sources.
- You will independently verify the ingredient list on the product packaging before consuming any product, especially where the consequences of error are significant to you.
