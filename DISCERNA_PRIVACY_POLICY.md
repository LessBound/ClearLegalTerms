## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Less Bound Pte. Ltd. ("**Less Bound**", "**we**", "**us**", or "**our**") operates the
Discerna mobile application ("**Discerna**" or "the **app**"). This policy explains what
information Discerna handles and how. It applies to Discerna on all platforms. It does not
apply to the separate legacy "Clear" application, which has its own policy, or to
third-party websites we link to.

## 2. What Discerna is for

Discerna reads food ingredient labels and checks the ingredients against dietary
restrictions you choose — ethics-based and faith-based preferences (for example
Muslim-friendly, vegan, vegetarian). Discerna reports on ingredients only; it does not
certify products.

**Discerna does not detect food allergens or medical-condition triggers.** It is not
designed for, and must not be relied on by, users with any allergy or medically restricted
dietary requirement.

## 3. Scanning happens on your device

By default, Discerna works entirely on your phone. When you scan a label, the camera image
is read and checked locally on the device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and the
app works fully offline.

## 4. Optional assist features you trigger

Two features send data to our server, and only when you tap them:

- **Second Look** sends the text of a single ingredient name so our server can research it
  and return a sourced answer.
- **Fresh Eyes** sends one photo of a label so our server can read it in full. Our server
  processes the photo and then discards it — we never store your photos on our servers. A
  copy of the photo stays on your own phone as part of your recent-scans list (see §7), and
  you can delete it at any time.

Both features show what will be sent before you confirm. If you never use them, nothing is
ever transmitted.

## 5. Anonymous identifier

To apply fair daily usage limits on the assist features, the app creates an anonymous
identifier (via Google's Firebase Authentication) the first time you use them. We do not
collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location, and there is
no account to sign up for.

## 6. Helping improve the ingredient database — only if you turn it on

Discerna is only as good as the ingredients it recognises. When a scan meets a word we do
not hold, that scan is the only evidence the gap exists.

**This is off unless you turn it on.** If you turn it on, the app sends a short summary of
how a scan went — never what the scan said.

**What a summary contains**

- how many ingredients were read, how many we could not identify, and how many were flagged
  for a restriction you have selected
- the answer shown for each restriction you have selected, and how confident the app was
- which text reader was used, and its version
- which script the label was written in, and whether you scanned again afterwards
- which restrictions you have selected
- the country or region the scan happened in

**What a summary never contains**

- the ingredients themselves, or any other text from the label
- the photograph, or any part of it
- your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location
- your IP address
- anything that would let us reconstruct a history of your scans

**A code that links your summaries together.** If you turn this on, we create a random code
for your device and attach it to each summary. It lets us tell one person scanning forty
times from forty people scanning once — without it we would be counting scans and guessing
at people.

That code is used **only** for these summaries. It is **not** the anonymous identifier
described in §5 that tracks your Second Look and Fresh Eyes allowance, and the two are
never joined.

**Which restriction you keep may say something about your religious or philosophical
beliefs.** That is sensitive information, and it is why this section asks for your
permission separately and plainly rather than folding it into anything else. You are free
to decline, and declining changes nothing else about how the app works.

**Your control over it**

- **Reset the code** at any time. Everything sent before the reset becomes unconnected to
  anything sent afterwards.
- **Ask us to delete everything held under your code**, at the address above, and we will.
- **Turn it off** at any time. That stops the sending and deletes anything still waiting on
  your device.

**How it behaves.** Summaries are held on your device and sent together, later. Scanning is
unaffected and continues to work with no connection at all.

**Why we ask.** The restrictions Discerna checks are matters of faith and of ethics. What we
learn from these summaries is used to widen the ingredient database and to show us where
labels are hard to read — which improves Discerna for the people who keep the same
restriction you do. We do not sell these summaries, and they are never used to change how a
verdict is reached.

## 7. What we keep

**On your phone.** Discerna keeps your recent scans on the device so you can look back at
them, and so you can ask for a Second Look or Fresh Eyes on a scan you have already done.
Each saved scan includes the photo you took and the result the app showed. Older scans are
removed automatically once the list is full. Nothing in this list is uploaded, and no one
else can see it. You can erase all of it at any time with **Clear scan data** in the app's
settings, and deleting the app removes it too.

**On our servers.** Ingredient answers produced by the server may be cached so that every
Discerna user benefits from them. These cached entries describe ingredients, not people, and
are not linked to you. Usage counters tied to the anonymous identifier reset daily. **Photos
are never retained on our servers.**

**Summaries under §6**, if you have turned them on, are kept for **90 days**. After that
they are combined into totals that are no longer held against any individual code.

## 8. Service providers

Server processing runs on Google Cloud and Firebase infrastructure (Singapore region), and
label reading and ingredient research use Google's Gemini API as a processor. We do not
sell your data, we do not show advertising, and we do not share your data with anyone for
marketing.

## 9. Your choices and rights

The assist features are optional; the core app is fully usable without them.
You can erase everything Discerna has saved on your phone — including the photos in your
recent-scans list — with **Clear scan data** in settings, without deleting the app. Deleting the
app removes all data stored on your device. If you have used the assist features and want
the usage records tied to your anonymous identifier removed, contact us at the address
above and we will delete them; note that we usually cannot connect an identifier to you
unless you provide it from your device.

The summaries described in §6 are off unless you turn them on. You can turn them off, reset
the code that links them, or ask us to delete everything held under that code, at any time.

## 10. Children

Discerna is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
data from them.
